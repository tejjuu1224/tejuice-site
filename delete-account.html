Delete your Tejuice account

Tejuice is an Android app made by Tejas (the developer). This page explains how to delete your account and the data in it.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes all data tied to it: your sign-in record, every save and its keyword, reminders and settings, your sync token, and any photos you added. Nothing is kept for later. Deleting cannot be undone.

If you still have the app

  1. Open Tejuice and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm.

The account and its data are deleted straight away, and the app also clears what it stored on your phone.

If you uninstalled the app

Email us from the Google account address you signed in with, and we will delete the account.

Email a deletion request

The subject is Delete my Tejuice account. We use the sender address to check the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days of your request and reply to confirm.

Usage statistics

If you left anonymous usage statistics on, they are tied only to a random id on your phone, not to your account or email, so we cannot find them by account. They contain no links, words, places or email. Deleting your account in the app makes the app forget that id. You can also turn statistics off in Settings before you delete.

Backups

Our database host, Supabase, may keep backups of the database. Any backup is kept for at most 7 days. Your data is not restored from a backup into the app after you delete it, and it is gone from every backup once that period ends.
