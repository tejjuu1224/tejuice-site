Tejuice privacy policy

Version 2026-11-09. Tejuice is an Android app made by Tejas (the developer), India.

What Tejuice does

You share a reel or link to Tejuice from another app, pick what it is (place, event, recipe, reference or later), and add a keyword if you like. Tejuice keeps these saves so you can find them again and plan them.

What we store, and why

  • Your Google sign-in details: your email address, name and profile picture link, as Google shares them. We use them to sign you in and keep your saves separate from everyone else's.
  • Your saves: the link you shared (as shared and a cleaned version), the app it came from, the kind you chose, and the keyword you typed. We use them to show your saves and to search them.
  • Your choices about each save: its status (to sort, confirmed, archived), snooze counts, reminder choice and date, and the date you opened it or marked it visited. We use them to remind you when you asked.
  • Places you lock: the Google place ID and the label you give it. We do not store the place's name, address, coordinates or photos.
  • Optional visit notes: a private mood word, a best-dish note and a photo, only if you add them.
  • Your settings: reminder preferences, your home area if you set one, and a record of when you accepted this policy and which consents you gave.
  • A sync token: a random code kept in your phone's secure storage so shares made offline can reach your account. Our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: counts of what happens in the app, such as how a share ended (saved, swiped away, cancelled), the kind and source app of a save, whether a keyword was added (yes or no, never the keyword), the number of results a search found (never what you typed), and taps like archive, restore or opening Settings. They are tied to a random id made on your phone, never to your account, email, links, words or places. We use them to decide which features to improve or remove. See "Consent and withdrawing it" for how to turn them off.

Tejuice does not read your clipboard on its own, does not track your location in the background, and does not collect your location in this version. We have no ads and we do not sell your data.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Our project is in the Mumbai (India) region.
  • PostHog receives the anonymous usage statistics above, on its EU cloud (servers in Frankfurt, Germany). Your data therefore leaves India for this purpose.
  • Google handles sign-in. When a feature needs it, Tejuice also sends a YouTube video ID or the text you type into a place search to Google (YouTube Data API, Places API) to get a title, thumbnail or place.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Consent and withdrawing it

Usage statistics are on by default. The app tells you about them once, on the Home screen, and nothing is sent until you have seen that notice. Tap "No thanks" there, or turn off "Share anonymous usage statistics" in Settings at any time, to stop them. Turning them off also throws away any that were waiting on your phone.

By signing in and using Tejuice you agree to the use of your data described here. Optional items, such as a visit photo, need your own action to be stored. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account in Settings or by emailing us. Without your data we cannot run the app, so withdrawing means your account is deleted.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it. Archived saves are kept until you delete the account.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Delete my account in the app, or follow the steps on the delete account page. Deleting removes all account data. Supabase may keep database backups for up to 7 days; deleted data is gone from them after that.

Usage statistics are not tied to your account, so they cannot be found or deleted by account. Deleting your account makes the app forget its random id, so nothing new can be linked to the old statistics. They hold no links, words, places or email.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected, have it erased, and nominate someone to act for you. Email us to use any of these.
